# Delight: character at the moments that earn it

A move of [refine mode](../refine.md). The shared preamble, verification rules and
next steps are there.

**Needs:** the brand's emotional range.

Delight is product character revealed through a useful interaction, a humane
response or an unexpectedly considered detail, at a moment that earns it. A layer
of generic whimsy is its opposite. It is the wrong move for motion whose job is
explaining state (animate), for a technical showpiece (overdrive), or for a surface
whose basics are not working yet (polish or harden first).

By mode ([modes](../../sf-frontend-ui/SKILL.md#modes)):
- **Persuade, Experience**: personality may run through voice, composition, motion
  and discovery, while the artifact stays the focus.
- **Operate, Read**: delight concentrates at first use, completion, recovery or
  mastery; reliability carries everything else.

[motion.md](../../sf-frontend-ui/references/motion.md),

## 1. Find the opportunity

Read the target, DESIGN.md, the product voice, how often each interaction repeats,
and its emotional context. Look for effort worth acknowledging, waiting that can
inform, an empty or first-use state that can orient, an error or recovery that
needs empathy, an interaction whose physical or verbal response could express the
brand, a useful capability worth discovering. An ordinary click gets no
celebration. Ask only when the emotional range or the stakes cannot be inferred.
Done when one moment is chosen and its emotional context named.

## 2. Write one delight thesis

One sentence: what the user should feel and why that feeling belongs to this
product. Then the smallest system that delivers it: a distinctive response to a
meaningful action; product-specific language that clarifies while carrying voice;
an interaction or transition with a recognisable material behaviour; an
illustration, sound, haptic or environmental detail from the product's world; a
discovery that reveals real utility. Derive it from the product's mechanism and
world, not a stock catalogue. Done when the thesis sentence and its one system are
written.

## 3. Build for the emotional moment

- **Success**: the response matches effort and consequence; a milestone may
  expand, a routine save simply feels certain.
- **Waiting**: truthful progress, useful context or product-specific activity;
  never faked work or a delayed completion staged for a flourish.
- **Empty and first use**: the next action is clear before personality is added.
- **Error and recovery**: the problem and the recovery lead; warmth may lower
  stress; loss, money, privacy and blocked work get no jokes.
- **Repeated interaction**: still satisfying on the hundredth use; variation only
  while it stays coherent enough to trust.
- **Discovery**: curiosity rewarded, required functionality never hidden.

Copy uses the product's language; neutral clarity beats generic whimsy.

## 4. Protect the experience

The delight leaves the primary task unblocked and fast, keeps platform conventions
and accessibility, adds no factual claims, plays sound only with consent and
honours mute, stays skippable and pleasant on repeat, and costs dependencies and
assets in proportion to the moment. It respects screen readers, keyboard, touch,
localisation and cultural context; nonessential loops stop when hidden;
celebration scales with frequency and consequence. Done when each of these holds
on the build.

## Done when

- The moment is specific enough that a neighbouring product could not use it unchanged.
- It improves comprehension, confidence, motivation or emotional recovery.
- The interface stays fast and obvious without the flourish.
- Repetition does not turn charm into friction.
- Muted, keyboard, touch and localised paths work.
- It feels like this product's world, and the personality feels earned.
