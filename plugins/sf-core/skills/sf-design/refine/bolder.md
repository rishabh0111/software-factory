# Bolder: raise one flat section

A move of [refine mode](../refine.md). The shared preamble, verification rules and
next steps are there.

**Needs:** which section is the target, and what must stay untouched.

"Bolder" amplifies something that already exists: the page, its system and its
conventions are the given, and one part rises to the conviction the rest already
implies. It is the wrong move while a direction decision is open: "bolder" said
then is that round's bolder register, a fresh deal of foreign forms, and belongs
to `/sf-design direction`. It is also the wrong move for a page with no world to
amplify (that is direction too). Reaching for more effects is the opposite of
bold; set that reflex aside first.

Craft depth: [the quality floor](../../sf-frontend-ui/SKILL.md#quality-floor),
[layout.md](../../sf-frontend-ui/references/layout.md),
[imagery.md](../../sf-frontend-ui/references/imagery.md).

## 1. Fix the scope

Touch only the named target. Its neighbours keep their styling, the page keeps its
idea, and the surface's colours, fonts, radii, shadows and primitives are the whole
palette. When the system genuinely cannot express the direction, ask with a
decision brief naming the exact addition and the job it would do, and wait. Done
when the target's boundary is named in one line and anything you would add beyond
the system has the user's yes.

## 2. Find why it reads flat

A flat section usually opts out of the system's own strongest moves. Compare it
with its neighbours: display type at full strength, the structural devices that
carry meaning, the signature motif, density and pacing. Done when you can name the
moves the neighbours make that this section skips.

## 3. Amplify

- **Amplify what the system owns.** Its motif and type scale at full strength,
  turned up for this section. The result looks more like the same brand.
- **Keep content true.** Existing claims stay unless the user supplies
  replacements; when real evidence the direction needs is absent, ask for it.
- **Commit, then clarify.** Make the one decisive move completely, then quiet what
  surrounds it so the move is legible. If every element got louder, the section got
  flatter.
- **Give it its own rhythm.** The target reads as a peak in the scroll: a shift in
  density or pace, not more of the same.

Done when one decisive move is fully built and its surroundings are quieted.

## 4. The skeleton test

Strip the copy from the section and study the bare structure. It should still say
what the section is and why it matters through hierarchy and the system's devices;
if it only works once the words return, the boldness is in the text size. An image
or artifact slot names a job, an anchor and a piece of evidence; fill it with what
the subject actually has. Done when the skeleton passes.

## Done when

- Everything outside the named target is unchanged.
- No new colour, font or primitive appeared without the user's yes.
- The section's conventions, including anything that drives an action, work as before.
- The section is unmistakably the same brand, more sure of itself, and holds its
  own without pulling the page apart.
